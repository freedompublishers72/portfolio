Four pieces of technical work, each showing the problem, what was built, and the engineering evidence behind it.

    A formal engineering workflow in which changes pass through verification gates, dedicated test harnesses, and certification records before they are considered complete.

Engineering approach

Production systems should be measurable, testable, recoverable, documented, secure, and maintainable. Work is verified against evidence — test harnesses, runtime behavior, and explicit verification gates — rather than assumed correct from a passing glance. Changes are not treated as complete until their behavior has been demonstrated.
